OUR PURPOSE · OUR SOURCES

About Little Lanterns

A learning journey guided by curiosity, gratitude and remembrance of Allah.

🕯️
🌱

Our creator, vision and mission

Created by Seyed Mohammad Abuzar Hashemi, a student of the Hawza in Qom.

Little Lanterns grew from a simple conviction: learning should never be separated from remembering the One who gives us knowledge. When a child learns a new word, reads a sentence or discovers an idea, that moment can also open a door to wonder, gratitude and a deeper connection with Allah.

Our vision is to nurture curious, thoughtful learners whose education strengthens their relationship with Allah, their love for the Qur’an and the Ahl al-Bayt (peace be upon them), and their commitment to beautiful character.

Our mission is to make language learning joyful and meaningful through stories, reading, speaking and reflection rooted in sound Islamic sources. We hope to help children grow in knowledge while understanding the purpose behind what they learn.

May every small lesson become a lantern: illuminating the mind, softening the heart and guiding each step toward what pleases Allah.

📜

The Sahifa al-Sajjadiyya

The core collection contains 54 numbered supplications attributed to Imam Ali ibn al-Husayn, Zayn al-Abidin (peace be upon him). The complete English text is presented separately from learning notes.

We do not silently rewrite the supplication into a child-friendly summary.

🤲

With gratitude and attribution

  • Imam Ali ibn al-Husayn Zayn al-Abidin (peace be upon him) — the supplications preserved in the Sahifa.
  • duas.org — English translation source and individual supplication pages.
  • The Rashed Foundation — distributor of the English-only Sahifa PDF edition.
  • Al-Islam.org — courtesy source credited on the duas.org pages and an important reference edition.

We are grateful to everyone who preserved, translated, hosted and shared these works. May Allah reward them with abundant thawab and accept their service.

🔎

How to read our sources

Original supplication: the English translation, kept distinct from commentary.

Learning notes: child-friendly explanations written for Little Lanterns; not the Imam’s words.

Memory rhymes: original learning aids; never quotations from the Imam.

Qur’anic connections: separately referenced. A dua is not presented as a standalone fiqh ruling.

A KIND REMINDER

Brave voices grow with practice

Speak slowly. Take a little pause when you need one. It is okay to make mistakes. Try again, learn a new word, and enjoy sharing your ideas.

Grown-ups: Give children time to finish. Listen first, praise their ideas, and gently model one improved sentence instead of correcting every mistake.

A note for grown-ups

Little Lanterns is an early visual prototype. Stories, syllable annotations, Qur’anic translations, audio and assessments will go through editorial review before being treated as finished learning material.

Children should use the site with an appropriate parent or teacher. Little Lanterns does not create child accounts or send learning progress to a server. Flag-learning progress is saved only in this browser on this device. The site does not record a child’s voice.
